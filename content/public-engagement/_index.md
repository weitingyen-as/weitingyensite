## Who Governs Taiwan (菜市場政治學)

Wei-Ting Yen is a co-founder and co-editor of
[*Who Governs Taiwan*](https://whogovernstw.org/), a Mandarin-language site that
explains political science research in plain language for readers in the
Chinese-speaking world. The project has also produced two edited volumes:
[*Who Governs?: Elections Edition*](https://www.books.com.tw/products/0010797101)
(2018), written for high school and college students and
[reviewed in China Channel](https://chinachannel.org/2019/08/05/who-governs/), and
[*Who Governs? EP2: Democratic Ark*](https://www.eslite.com/product/1001141162682134504005)
(2022).

You can also follow the project on its
[Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/whogovernstw), or read
[her author archive](http://whogovernstw.org/category/wei-ting-yen/).
