## On teaching

Her chapter [*In Unity There is Strength: How to Incorporate Your Research into
Teaching*](../publication/in-unity-there-is-strength/), in *The Palgrave Handbook of
Teaching and Research in Political Science*, sets out how she folds an active
research agenda into undergraduate courses.

She also lectures for the Ministry of Education's programme for cultivating high
school talent in the humanities and social sciences, most recently on how
democracies emerge and die.
