intro: |
  Wei-Ting Yen is a political economist at the Institute of Political Science, Academia Sinica, where she studies how states in Asia build — and fail to build — protection against economic risk. Her work runs from the micro-foundations of welfare states in young democracies to the state capacity that governments drew on during COVID-19. She is a co-founder of *Who Governs Taiwan*, a Mandarin-language site that brings political science research to a general readership.
