intro: |
  Wei-Ting Yen is an Assistant Research Fellow at the Institute of Political Science, Academia Sinica, studying democratic governance, comparative political economy, and welfare state development in Asia. A public intellectual on Taiwan's democracy and Taiwanese public opinion toward U.S.-China-Taiwan relations, she is a Public Intellectuals Program Fellow at the National Committee on U.S.-China Relations, writes regularly for the Brookings Institution, *Foreign Affairs*, and *Foreign Policy*, and is frequently interviewed by the *New York Times*, the *Washington Post*, and the *Wall Street Journal*. She holds a Ph.D. in Political Science from The Ohio State University.
intro_zh: |
  顏維婷為中央研究院政治學研究所助研究員，研究亞洲的民主治理、比較政治經濟學與福利國家發展。作為公共知識分子，她專注於台灣民主治理，以及台灣民眾對美中台三方關係的看法；為美中關係全國委員會公共知識分子計畫成員，長期於布魯金斯研究院、《外交事務》與《外交政策》發表評論，並經常接受《紐約時報》、《華盛頓郵報》、《華爾街日報》等國際媒體採訪。她於俄亥俄州立大學取得政治學博士學位。
