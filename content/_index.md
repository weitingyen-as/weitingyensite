  Wei-Ting Yen is a political economist at the Institute of Political Science, Academia Sinica. Her research focuses on comparative political economy, democratic governance, and welfare state development in Asia. Beyond her academic research, she is actively engaged in public discourse, bringing Taiwanese perspectives to international discussions of U.S.–China–Taiwan relations. She regularly contributes commentary and analysis to international media and policy outlets.
